Approach & preparation

01 / Inspect the data

Reviewed sample records, column types, descriptive statistics, missing values, and unique values. The original inspection identified 11 missing postal codes and 18 input columns.

02 / Transform & summarize

Parsed order and shipment dates using day/month/year, calculated order-to-shipment days, extracted order year, removed the country and exported row-number columns, and grouped sales by geography and year.

Interpretation & limitations

  • Regional sales concentration can guide further investigation, but does not establish where additional investment will be profitable.
  • Order rows are line items: the dataset contains 9,800 rows and 4,922 distinct order IDs.
  • The export reports 49 unique state-level entries, including the District of Columbia; it does not cover every U.S. state.
  • Sales measure dollar value, not units sold. Profit, margin, and causal explanations are not established by this analysis.
  • The original notebook also explored correlations and order-to-shipment timing; no conclusions about those relationships are asserted here.

This presentation uses the saved outputs in the original Markdown export. The underlying CSV was not supplied, so the full analysis was not rerun. Regional, annual, and state sales totals were checked for consistency.
